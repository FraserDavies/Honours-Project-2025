---                                                                                                                                                                           User Testing Script — Gantt Chart Builder                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   Before You Start                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
- Have the app running and the login page open
- Advise participant: "Please think out loud as you go - say what you're looking at, what you expect to happen, and what confuses you."

---
Step 1: Login

1. Direct participant to the login page
2. Ask participant to log in using the provided email credentials (fd2010@hw.ac.uk)

Observe: Is the form clear? Does the participant know where to type?

---
Step 2: Project Selection

1. After login, participant should see a project list
2. Ask participant to find and open Project 1001

Observe: Is it obvious how to select a project? Do they click correctly?

---
Step 3: Reading the Gantt Chart

1. Once the chart loads, ask participant to identify:
- A specific task by name
- The start/end date of a task
- Any task dependencies (connecting lines)
- Navigate to today's date is on the chart

Observe: Can they interpret the chart without help?

---
Step 4: Hovering / Tooltips

1. Ask participant to hover over a task bar

Observe: Do they notice the tooltip? Is the information useful?

---
Step 5: Enabling Edit Mode

1. Ask: "If you wanted to make changes to a task, where would you start?"
2. Let them find the edit mode toggle themselves

Observe: Is the edit mode button discoverable? Do they understand what it does?

---
Step 6: Rename a Task

1. In edit mode, ask them to change the name of a task
2. They should click on the task label/bar to trigger the rename input

Observe: Is it clear how to rename? Is the input easy to use?

---
Step 7: Move a Task (Drag to Change Dates)

1. Ask them to move a task to a different date by dragging it

Observe:
- Do they attempt to drag? Is the drag handle obvious?
- Do they understand the task has snapped to a new date after releasing?
- Do they look for confirmation that the change was saved?

---
Step 8: Resize a Task (Change Duration)

1. Ask them to try extending or shortening a task's duration

Observe: Do they find the resize handle on the edge of the bar?

---
Step 9: Exiting Edit Mode / Saving

1. Ask them to stop editing and return to the normal view

Observe: Is it clear how to exit edit mode? Do they expect a "Save" button?

---
Step 10: Wrap-up Questions

After the test, ask:
- "What was the most confusing part?"
- "Was there anything you expected to find that wasn't there?"
- "On a scale of 1–5, how confident did you feel using the chart?"
- "Is there anything you'd change?"
